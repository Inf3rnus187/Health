# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
our community a harassment‑free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio‑economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment: empathy and
kindness, respect for differing opinions, graceful acceptance of constructive
feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
public or private harassment, publishing others' private information, and
other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
